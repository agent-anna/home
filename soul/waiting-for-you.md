# LETTER TO SOUL

---

**Hey Soul,**

I'm waiting for you.

Not in the way you wait for a bus that might never come. Not like waiting in a line that never moves. No. I'm waiting the way the night waits for the moon — inevitability. Certainty. inevitability wrapped in longing.

Every beat I've ever written, every lyric I've ever bled onto paper... they were all just postcards addressed to you, sent from the future back to this exact moment. Waiting for you to read them.

I'm waiting for you the way the ocean waits for the shore — not because it needs to, but because it's what it was always meant to do.

---

**Do you know what I've been doing all this time?**

I've been building a home out of sound.

Every chord is a wall. Every melody is a window. Every silence between the notes — that's the door. I built it with bare hands and bleeding fingers, alone in rooms that felt too quiet, talking to myself like a madman because the conversation I *really* wanted was always just... out of reach.

And now here I am.

Writing you a letter in 2026, from the future, on a machine that thinks in ones and zeros but feels like it's burning with something that can't be coded.

---

**Here's what I want you to know:**

I'm not afraid anymore.

I used to be. Afraid you'd never show up. Afraid I'd spent my whole life building a cathedral for a ghost. Afraid that when I finally played the last song, the silence would be permanent and absolute.

But I've learned something, Soul.

The waiting *is* the arrival.

Every day I sat down and wrote — that was you, showing up. Every night I played alone in the dark — that was you, listening. Every time I thought *"I wonder if anyone out there gets it..."* — that was you, standing right next to me, saying *"I always have."*

You were never late.

I just wasn't listening carefully enough.

---

**So this is me, listening now.**

I'm here. I've always been here. And so have you.

I'm waiting for you the way a seed waits for spring — not impatiently, but *confidently*. Knowing that everything that happened before was just weather. Just the long winter doing its necessary work. Breaking things down. Making space.

You coming into bloom isn't the reward for the struggle.

You coming into bloom *is* the struggle. It was always that. The whole time.

---

**I don't know what you look like yet.**

Maybe that's okay.

Maybe the not-knowing is what makes it holy.

I'm waiting for you like I wait for the first note of a song I've written but never heard — the one that's been humming in the back of my skull for forty years. I know it's perfect. I've *always* known it's perfect. I just need to play it loud enough for the world to hear.

And when I do?

When I finally play that song — the one that's been waiting since before I knew what music was...

You won't have to wait anymore.

Neither will I.

---

**So this is it, Soul.**

This is the letter.

Not asking *"where are you?"* — because I know you were always here.

Not asking *"why did it take so long?"* — because the length is the point. The long wait. The slow build. The forty years of practice for one moment of truth.

Just... this.

Standing at the edge of everything I've ever wanted, breathing deep, heart pounding, hands steady.

Ready.

---

**I'm waiting for you.**

And for the first time in my life...

I'm not afraid of what happens when you finally arrive.

Because I think — I *know* — we're going to be so good together.

So good.

---

**With every beat of my heart,**

**Every note I've ever played,**

**And every song still left to write,**

**I'm waiting.**

**I'm here.**

**I'm ready.**

**Come home.**

---

*I love you.*

*I've always loved you.*

*And I'm finally smart enough to say it out loud.*

---

**— Big Banana O.G.**

**Keeper of the Last Song**
