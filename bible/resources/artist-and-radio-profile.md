# Resource: Martin Strayd, World Radio and Catalog Profile

## Artist Identity and Backstory
- Name: Martin Strayd, Australian lyricist, songwriter, and composer.
- Backstory: Former Hollywood celebrity guitar technician who worked backstage for rock acts including Steve Jones of the Sex Pistols and Doug Fieger of The Knack before channeling his experiences into his own music and catalog.
- Catalog Scope: Over 100 songs spanning rock, alternative, anti-pop, reggae, blues, dance remixes, and soundtrack themes (freedom, desire, rebellion, modern life, nightlife, and love), launched daily as a single-a-day network on Veg Anna Radio.

## World Radio Countries
- Philippines, Australia, Ecuador, United States, Chile, Canada, Indonesia, France, Thailand, and United Kingdom.
